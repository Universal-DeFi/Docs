Before you list a token, set up the custodian that holds its backing and the credentials the platform uses to sign onchain and verify agreements.

## Custodians

**Custodians** lets you manage custodians and the asset classes they support.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a custodian">
    Open **Custodians** in your organization workspace and select **Add custodian**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Name**. A **Slug** is generated from it, and you can edit it. Add a **Region** if you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set its capabilities">
    Select **Add asset** for each asset the custodian holds, for example silver, and pick the settlement methods it supports.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Custodians confirm the reserve through attestations: signed reserve statements and bar lists that you upload to the token. The platform treats attestations older than 90 days as stale, so upload a fresh one regularly.

## Credentials

**Credentials** connects the providers your tokens use. Select **Add credential** and pick a provider.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Signing provider: Fireblocks" icon="signature">
    Connect your Fireblocks workspace for mint and burn signing. Choose your workspace's region, enter the **API key**, and upload the matching private key file. Once a mint or burn passes approval, the platform signs and broadcasts it through this connection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Agreement provider: DocuSign" icon="file-signature">
    Verify signed agreements through DocuSign. Register one DocuSign credential; issuance and redemption requests that require an agreement are checked against this account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Secrets you enter are encrypted in your browser before they're sent.

To remove a credential, select **Revoke**. A token that still signs or verifies with a credential must be moved to another one first.

<Warning>
  Only add credentials scoped to the vaults and accounts your tokens need. Rotate them in Fireblocks or DocuSign if you think they've been exposed, then revoke the old credential here.
</Warning>
