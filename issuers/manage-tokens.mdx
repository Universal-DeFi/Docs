**Tokens** lists your organization's tokens. Open one to manage it.

## Token tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Supply, asset, custodian, contract, and status. |
| **Reserve** | The reserve backing the token, including bars held for commodity tokens, and its attestations. |
| **Request settings** | Fees, increments, settlement methods, and who can file requests. |
| **Requests** | Every issuance and redemption request on the token. |
| **On-chain** | Contract details and onchain activity. |

## Keep the reserve current

For commodity tokens, the token page compares onchain supply with the reserve the custodian holds. Upload fresh reserve attestations regularly; the platform treats attestations older than 90 days as stale.

## Suspend, resume, or delist

- **Suspend** stops new issuance and redemption requests on the token. Holdings are untouched.
- **Resume** opens the token for requests again.
- **Delist** permanently removes the token from the platform. A delisted token can't be resumed.

Depending on your policies, these actions may need approval from an approval group.

<Warning>
  Delisting is permanent. If you only need to pause activity, suspend the token instead.
</Warning>

## Public page

Every listed token has a public page on [Listed tokens](https://app.universalmint.io/listed-tokens) showing its contract, supply, reserve, documents, terms, and mint and burn history. Investors and the public can see it without signing in.
