**Investor requests** lists the mint and burn requests investors filed against your tokens. Open one to review it and advance the workflow.

Each step belongs to a role you assigned when you [listed the token](/issuers/list-a-token). If a step belongs to someone else, the request is read-only for you until it reaches your step.

## Issuance requests

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register the deposit (operator)">
    When the custodian has received the asset, upload the **Deposit confirmation** and enter each bar's serial number and weight, or upload a CSV or PDF bar list. Select **Confirm deposit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the mint (mint approver)">
    Review the request and the checks, then approve. If the mint approver is an approval group, each member co-approves until the threshold is met.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mint onchain">
    Once approved, the mint is signed through your signing provider and broadcast. The request shows **Minted on-chain** with the transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For ASLV, the evidence is reviewed in two stages before the mint can be approved:

1. Transfer evidence: evidence of the silver's transfer into the reserve is uploaded, reviewed, and approved.
2. Deposit evidence: the deposit confirmation, the updated holdings, and the bar serialization file are uploaded, reviewed, and approved.

Mint approval follows approval of both evidence stages.

## Redemption requests

BMAX return sales follow a separate arrangement with approved counterparties. The steps below describe redemption for tokens that provide holder redemption rights.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm with the investor (account manager)">
    Confirm the investor's identity, the amount, the delivery method, and the destination, and quote the fee. Attach the call recording and select **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the investor's transfers">
    The investor sends the tokens to the Token Return Wallet and, if a fee is charged, sends it separately to the Fee Wallet. They enter the hash for each transfer, which the request checks onchain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the release (operator)">
    Record the custodian's release with its evidence. For physical delivery, enter the FedEx tracking number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the burn (burn approver)">
    Review and approve the burn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Burn onchain">
    The returned tokens are burned. The request shows **Burned & settled on-chain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm delivery (operator)">
    Upload proof of delivery to complete the request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checks before signing

Before a mint or burn can be signed, the request shows a set of checks:

- **Verification**: the investor's identity is current.
- **Address screening (KYT)**: the destination wallet passed screening.
- **Destination not on denylist**
- **Token transfer** and **Fee payment**: the investor's transfers were found onchain, for redemptions.

If a check hasn't passed, the request shows **This request cannot be signed** until it does.

## Investors

**Investors** lists the people and organizations that have filed requests on your tokens, with their identity status and wallets.
