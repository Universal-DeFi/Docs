You don't need an account to look at the tokens on Universal Mint or what their issuers have disclosed.

## Listed tokens

[Listed tokens](https://app.universalmint.io/listed-tokens) shows every token on the platform. Open a token to see:

- Its issuer, asset, custodian, contract address, and decimals.
- Its total supply.
- For commodity tokens, a **Reserve backing** card comparing supply with the reserve, and the bars held in the vault by serial number and weight.
- Public documents, such as the whitepaper and the token's terms.

## Onchain transactions

You can view a token's mint and burn transactions by looking up its contract address in the [mainnet explorer](https://ault.explorer.orynt.tech/).

## Disclosures

[Disclosures](https://app.universalmint.io/transparency) lists notices published by issuers. Filter by type:

| Type | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Attestations** | Confirmations of the reserve backing a token. |
| **Lifecycle** | Events in the life of the asset or the token. |
| **Incidents** | Operational problems and how they were handled. |
| **Policy** | Changes to the policies that govern a token. |

## Platform status

A platform pause restricts new requests and further actions on existing requests, including transfer hash submissions and approvals. Existing balances and records remain unchanged.

<Tip>
  Issuers: learn how to publish a disclosure in [Disclosures](/issuers/disclosures).
</Tip>
