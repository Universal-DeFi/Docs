## Security

Open the **Profile** menu and select **Security** to manage:

- **Signing keys**: your passkeys and recovery key. See [Signing keys](/account/signing-keys).
- **Password**: change your password. Accounts that sign in with Google or Microsoft don't have one.
- **Active sessions**: see the devices signed in to your account, and select **Sign out others** to end every session except this one.
- **Recent sign-ins**: review when and where your account was used.

<Tip>
  If you see a sign-in you don't recognize, select **Sign out others**, change your password, and contact [support@universalmint.io](mailto:support@universalmint.io).
</Tip>

## Activity

**Activity** in your **Profile** shows the actions you've taken across the app, with who signed each one and its outcome.

## Invitations

**Invitations** in your **Profile** lists invitations to join organizations. Select **Accept** or **Decline**.

## Notifications

The bell in the header shows requests moving, approvals waiting on you, and decisions that came back. Select **Mark all read** to clear them.

In an organization workspace, a second bell shows **Approvals** waiting for your vote. See [Approval groups](/issuers/approval-groups).
