Tokens are minted to, and redeemed from, wallets you register. You need at least one verified wallet before you can request tokens.

## Register a wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Wallets">
    In your **Personal** workspace, select **Wallets** under **Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the address">
    Select **Register an address**. Enter the **Wallet address** (starting with `0x`), an optional **Label**, and the **Network** your tokens live on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prove you own it">
    Choose how to sign the ownership message:
    - **Browser wallet**: sign with a browser wallet extension such as MetaMask or Rainbow.
    - **Signed elsewhere**: copy the message, sign it in your wallet, and paste the signature back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Signing proves you control the address. It's a message, not a transaction: there's no gas fee and nothing moves.

Once verified, the wallet shows **Verified**. Wallets are also screened automatically before use and show **KYT cleared** when they pass.

## Manage wallets

- **Set primary**: choose the wallet that receives tokens by default.
- **Sign to verify**: finish verifying a wallet you added but didn't sign for.

## Organization wallets

Organizations register **Treasury wallets** on their **Organization** page. The primary treasury wallet receives minted tokens and is the source for redemptions. Organizations can also connect a **Custody vault** instead of a browser wallet.

<Warning>
  Only Ethereum-style addresses (`0x` followed by 40 characters) are supported. Double-check the address before you sign. Tokens sent to the wrong address can't be recovered by Universal Mint.
</Warning>
