You need to verify your identity before you can file issuance or redemption requests. Identity and source-of-funds checks are handled by our verification partner, Persona.

## Verify your identity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open identity verification">
    Open the **Profile** menu and select **Identity verification**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the check">
    Select **Verify with Persona**. Persona asks for a photo ID and a selfie, and usually takes a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the result">
    When you finish, the page shows **Your documents are submitted** and updates by itself once the check is decided.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you leave partway through, select **Resume verification** to pick up where you stopped.

## Statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Unverified** | You haven't completed verification yet. |
| **Verified** | Your identity is verified. The page shows the date it's valid until. |
| **Expired** | Your verification has lapsed. Select **Verify again with Persona** to renew it. |

If Persona declines a verification, you'll see **Persona declined this verification**. Select **Start a new verification** to try again, and make sure your ID is valid and the photos are clear.

## Business verification

Organizations go through a separate business verification (KYB). See [Create an organization](/issuers/create-an-organization).

<Note>
  Until your identity is verified, your name shows **(Unverified)** in the app, and request forms show **Identity verification is required before filing a request.**
</Note>
