As an investor, you can request tokens backed by real assets, hold them in your own wallet, and redeem them for the underlying asset. Set up these four things first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account" icon="user-plus">
    Sign up, register your passkey, and save your recovery phrase. See [Create an account](/get-started/create-an-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your identity" icon="id-card">
    Complete identity verification with Persona. You can't file requests until it's verified. See [Identity verification](/account/identity-verification).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register a wallet" icon="wallet">
    Add and verify the wallet that will receive your tokens. See [Wallets](/account/wallets).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a delivery address (optional)" icon="truck">
    If you plan to redeem commodity tokens for physical metal, add the address it should ship to. See [Delivery addresses](/account/delivery-addresses).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find a token

Open **Listed tokens** to see every token on the platform. Each token's page shows its issuer, custodian, supply, reserve, fees, terms, and documents. Read the token's terms and documents before you request it.

## Investing as an organization

If you invest on behalf of a company, request an organization and switch to it from the **Workspace** menu. Requests filed in the organization's workspace use the organization's verified business details and treasury wallets. See [Create an organization](/issuers/create-an-organization).

## Holding tokens

Your tokens live onchain in your own wallet. Your **Overview** shows your balances across your registered wallets and the latest disclosures from issuers.
