<Card title="Email support" icon="envelope" href="mailto:support@universalmint.io">
  support@universalmint.io
</Card>

## What to include

To help us answer quickly, include:

- The email address on your Universal Mint account.
- The request reference, if your question is about a specific request.
- The token symbol, if it's about a specific token.
- Any transaction hash you've already sent.
- A screenshot of any error message.

<Warning>
  Universal Mint staff will never ask for your password, your recovery phrase, or your wallet's private key. Don't share them with anyone.
</Warning>
