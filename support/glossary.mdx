title: "Glossary"
description: "The terms you'll see in Universal Mint."
icon: "book"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Approval group">
    A set of people, or other approval groups, who approve an action together. Each group has a threshold such as **2 of 3 required**. See [Approval groups](/issuers/approval-groups).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Asset-backed token">
    A token whose supply is backed by an asset held by a custodian, such as silver bars in a vault.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Attestation">
    A statement, usually from a custodian, confirming the reserve that backs a token. Attestations older than 90 days are treated as stale.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Burn">
    Removing tokens from circulation when they're redeemed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Custodian">
    The party that holds the asset backing a token.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Delegation">
    Permission for a person or approval group to act for your organization on specific actions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Denylist">
    The platform's list of participants and wallet addresses blocked from onboarding and new requests.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Disclosure">
    A public notice from an issuer: an attestation, an asset lifecycle event, an incident, or a policy change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Investor">
    A person or organization that requests, holds, or redeems tokens.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Issuance">
    A request to mint new tokens to an investor's wallet. Also called a mint request.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Issuer">
    An organization approved to list tokens on Universal Mint.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="KYB">
    Know your business: verification of an organization's legal and ownership details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="KYC">
    Know your customer: verification of a person's identity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Listing">
    An issuer's request to offer a token on the platform. Approving a listing creates the token.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mint">
    Creating new tokens, backed by assets held in custody.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Passkey">
    A sign-in credential stored on your device and unlocked with Touch ID, Windows Hello, or a security key.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Platform halt">
    An emergency control that pauses all new requests across the platform. Balances and data are unchanged.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Proposal">
    An action waiting for approval from an approval group. Its details are fixed when it opens.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Recovery phrase">
    A list of 12 or 24 words that lets you recover your account if you lose your passkey.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Redemption">
    A request to return tokens for the underlying asset. Also called a burn request.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reserve">
    The assets a custodian holds to back a token's supply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Slug">
    An organization's or approval group's short name, used in links and invitations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Workspace">
    The view you're working in: your personal account or one of your organizations. Switch between them from the workspace menu.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
