## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to [app.universalmint.io](https://app.universalmint.io).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Enter your email and password and select **Sign in**, or select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** if you signed up that way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve your passkey">
    The passkey prompt opens automatically. Approve it with Touch ID, Windows Hello, or your security key. If it doesn't open, select **Use passkey**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You stay signed in on that browser until you select **Sign out** from the **Profile** menu, or until you sign out other sessions from [Security settings](/account/settings-and-notifications).

<Tip>
  Lost the device with your passkey? Select **Lost your device? Recover with your recovery phrase**. See [Passkeys and recovery](/account/passkeys-and-recovery).
</Tip>

## Reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a code">
    On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and select **Send code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Enter the 6-character code from your email. It expires in 10 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new password">
    Enter a new password and select **Update password**. Sessions open on other devices stop working once you save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you signed up with Google or Microsoft, there's no password to reset. Use the **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** button instead.

## Switch workspaces

If you belong to an organization, the **Workspace** menu at the top of the sidebar lets you switch between your **Personal** workspace and each organization.

- Your **Personal** workspace shows your own holdings, requests, wallets, and delivery addresses.
- An **organization** workspace shows that organization's tokens, requests, members, and approvals.

The app remembers the last workspace you used. Organizations awaiting approval appear under **Pending review**. To apply for a new one, select **Add organization**.

## Common sign-in messages

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No account for that email: create one first.** | Check the spelling, or [create an account](/get-started/create-an-account). |
| **That email is already registered with a different sign-in method.** | Sign in with the method you used before, such as Google or Microsoft. |
| **That sign-in expired. Start again.** | Go back to the sign-in page and try again. |
| **Couldn't reset your password. The code may have expired.** | Request a new code. Codes last 10 minutes. |
