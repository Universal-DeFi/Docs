You need an account to request or redeem tokens, or to set up an issuer organization. Signing up takes a few minutes.

## Before you start

- A device that supports passkeys: Touch ID or Face ID on a Mac or iPhone, Windows Hello on a PC, or a hardware security key.
- A safe place to write down a 12 or 24 word recovery phrase.

## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up page">
    Go to [app.universalmint.io/signup](https://app.universalmint.io/signup). You can also select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** on the sign-in page to use an existing work account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Enter **Your name**, **Email**, and a **Password**, then confirm the password and select **Create account**. Passwords need at least 8 characters, with a number and a special character.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email">
    Enter the 6-character code we sent to your email and select **Continue**. The code expires in 10 minutes. If it didn't arrive, select **Didn't get it? Resend code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register your passkey">
    Select **Register passkey** and approve the prompt on your device. Every account signs in with a passkey; it becomes your second factor on every sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery phrase">
    Choose **12 words** or **24 words** and select **Generate recovery phrase**. Copy or download it, store it safely offline, and select **Confirm phrase saved**. Then re-enter the three words we ask for and select **Confirm and continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the platform terms">
    Read the **Platform terms**, tick the required clauses, and select **Agree and continue**. Optional clauses can be left unticked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Your recovery phrase is the only way back into your account if you lose your passkey. We can't recover it for you, and Universal Mint staff will never ask for it.
</Warning>
